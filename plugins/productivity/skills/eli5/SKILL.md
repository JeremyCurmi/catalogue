# ELI5

The reader did not follow the last answer. Throw the explanation away and build a new one.

This is not a shorter version — `summarize` does that. Here the first attempt failed, so start over from what the reader already knows. The result may be longer than the original.

## How

1. **Name the one thing** the answer was actually about, in a single sentence, before any detail.
2. **Start from familiar ground** — a real-world analogy, or a concept already used in this conversation. Then map it onto the actual thing, piece by piece.
3. **Build up.** Simple case first, then the complication, then the edge case. Never all at once.
4. **Kill every unexplained term.** First use of a technical word gets a plain-English definition in the same sentence, or gets replaced.
5. **Say why it matters** — what breaks, what it unlocks, what the reader would do with it.

## Rules

- Keep the substance. Names, numbers, decisions and warnings survive; only the wording changes.
- Analogies must be honest — say where they break down if it matters.
- Do not repeat the failed phrasing. Different words, different angle.
- Do not add new claims or advice that were not in the original.
- Do not apologise or narrate ("let me try that again"). Just explain.

## Output

Plain prose. Short paragraphs, short sentences. Bullets only for a genuine list. End with one sentence naming the takeaway.
